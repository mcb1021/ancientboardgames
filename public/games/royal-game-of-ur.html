How to play Royal Game of Ur

  1. Each player has 7 pieces that start off the board. The board has 20 squares, and 5 of them are rosettes.
  2. Roll four pyramid-shaped dice and count the marked tips facing up. That gives you 0 to 4. A 0 means you lose your turn.
  3. Move one piece forward by the number you rolled. You can't land on your own piece.
  4. Land on an opponent and their piece goes back to the start. Pieces sitting on a rosette are safe.
  5. Landing on a rosette also gives you another roll.
  6. You need the exact number to bear a piece off. First to get all 7 off wins.

A little history

In the 1920s Sir Leonard Woolley dug up the Royal Cemetery of Ur in what is now Iraq. He found several game boards buried around 2600 BCE, and the best known one sits in the British Museum today.

Nobody knew the rules until 1980, when British Museum curator Irving Finkel read a Babylonian clay tablet from 177 BCE. A scribe named Itti-Marduk-balatu had written them down.
