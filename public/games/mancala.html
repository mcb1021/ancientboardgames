How to play Mancala

  1. The board has 12 small pits, 6 per player, plus a big store at each end. Every small pit starts with 4 seeds. Your store is the one on your right.
  2. On your turn, pick up all the seeds from one of your pits and drop one in each pit going counter-clockwise.
  3. Drop one in your own store as you pass it. Skip your opponent's store.
  4. If your last seed lands in your store, go again.
  5. If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture it plus every seed in the pit straight across.
  6. When one side runs out, the other player keeps what's left on their side. Most seeds wins.

A little history

Mancala isn't really one game. It's a family of more than 800 games played across Africa, the Middle East and Asia, and the sowing move looks a lot like planting a field.

You'll find Oware in West Africa, Bao in East Africa, Congkak in Southeast Asia and Kalah in America. African mancala games start showing up in written records around 700 CE, and they're likely much older.
